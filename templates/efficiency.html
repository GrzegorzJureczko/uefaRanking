📊 How to read this

Each cell is the 5-year UEFA points total of the country's clubs that qualified for European competitions, divided by the 5-year UEFA points total of all the country's clubs in the UEFA ranking that season. A club counts as qualified if it played in a UEFA competition the following season (so cup winners are included and clubs banned from Europe are not). The current season has no following season yet, so there the clubs in the European spots of the league tables are counted, out of the clubs in those tables. A low value means a large part of the federation's points sits with clubs that did not qualify for European competitions. Hover a cell for the underlying point totals. Click a season header to leave it out of (or put it back into) the Avg column; use the arrow next to a header to sort.
